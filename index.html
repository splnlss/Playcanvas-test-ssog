Controls & scene info

Orbit: left drag to rotate, right drag to pan, wheel to zoom.

Fly: select Fly, then click the scene. Mouse looks; WASD moves; Q/E moves down/up. Shift boosts speed. Escape releases the mouse.

VR: left stick flies horizontally; right stick moves up/down or snap-turns. Left X opens/closes the menu and pauses flight. Point the right controller at a button and press its trigger. Menu includes Reset, Exit, and quality/FPS controls.

Flight passes through geometry. Walking collision and avatars are planned separately. VR quality chooses the next headset preset; desktop uses its configured budget. This viewer renders on the device opening the page.
